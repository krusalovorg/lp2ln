# Contributing to LP2LN

Thank you for your interest in contributing to LP2LN.

By contributing to LP2LN, you agree that:

1. You have the right to submit your contribution.
2. Your contribution may be distributed under the GNU AGPLv3.
3. Your contribution may be used in both open-source and commercial versions of LP2LN.
4. The project owner may also use, relicense, and distribute your contribution as part of LP2LN under commercial licensing terms.
5. You do not transfer copyright ownership unless separately agreed in writing.

## Contributor License Agreement

Significant contributions may require acceptance of the LP2LN Contributor
License Agreement in `CLA.md`.

Do not submit code, documentation, or other materials unless you are allowed to
license them under these terms.

## Development

Before opening a pull request, please:

- ensure the project builds;
- run relevant tests;
- describe the purpose and impact of your change;
- disclose any third-party code or generated content included in the contribution.
